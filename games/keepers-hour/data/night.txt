voices: lens 3, ledger 4, tide 2, static 1

== arrive
LETTER: By order of the Coastal Authority, the light at Relay Nine is to be extinguished at dawn. There is no longer a coast to guard.
TIDE: There is a coast. You are standing on it. It just has no sea.
LEDGER: Dawn is at 06:12. That gives us the night. Paperwork first.
LENS: The lamp is turning. It has not stopped in eleven years. Go and look at it.
* Put the letter down. -> END

== lamp
LENS: Four seconds a sweep. The beam crosses the north window, the stairwell, your hands, the north window.
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine, Relay Nine, we see you... kssh...
* Look into the lens. -> lamp_look
* [LEDGER 9] Recite the maintenance schedule from memory. -> lamp_schedule | lamp_schedule_fail
* [STATIC 10] Listen to whoever just said that. -> lamp_voice | lamp_voice_fail
* Step back. -> END

== lamp_look
LENS: Three hundred and twelve facets of pressed glass. Each one holds a smaller lamp, turning.
LENS: In the middle there is a white point you cannot look at for long. You look at it for long.
TIDE: It is not looking back. Probably.
* Count the facets. -> lamp_count
* Look away. -> lamp

== lamp_count
LENS: You lose count at forty. You start again. You lose count at forty.
LEDGER: The number is three hundred and twelve. It is written on a brass plate. You polished the plate on Tuesday.
* Fine. -> lamp

== lamp_schedule
LEDGER: Wick trimmed daily. Lens wiped at dusk. Clockwork wound every four hours. Log signed at each winding.
LEDGER: You have done all of these, every night, for eleven years, for nobody. That is not nothing. That is a record.
* It is a record. -> lamp

== lamp_schedule_fail
LEDGER: Wick... daily. Lens... at some point. Something about the clockwork.
LEDGER: We had it written down. We always have it written down. The logbook will know.
* Ask the logbook later. -> lamp

== lamp_voice
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine. This is the Marigold, out of nowhere, bound for nowhere. We have been following your light for a long time...
STATIC: ...please do not... kssh... we would not know where we were.
TIDE: There is no Marigold. There has not been a ship here since the water went. And yet.
* "Marigold, this is Relay Nine. I hear you." -> lamp_marigold
* Turn the radio down in your head. -> lamp

== lamp_voice_fail
STATIC: ...kssh... kssh... a shipping forecast for a sea that is not there. Fair, becoming poor. Good, occasionally moderate.
* Let it fade. -> lamp

== lamp_marigold
STATIC: ...kssh...
LENS: Nothing answers. The beam crosses your hands again.
TIDE: You said it out loud. That counts for something. Tell the logbook.
* Step back. -> END

== logbook
LOGBOOK: Ahem. Relay Nine, keeper's log, volume eleven. You are holding me upside down.
LENS: The last entry is in your handwriting. It says "Nothing." in careful capitals.
* Turn it the right way up. -> logbook_open
* [TIDE 8] Ask it how it is. -> logbook_feelings | logbook_feelings_fail
* Close it. -> END

== logbook_open
LOGBOOK: Four thousand and seven entries. Four thousand and six of them say "Nothing." One of them says "Gull."
LEDGER: The gull was in the spring of the third year. We were very excited about the gull.
* Read the gull entry. -> logbook_gull
* Write tonight's entry. -> logbook_write

== logbook_gull
LOGBOOK: "03:40. Gull on the gallery rail. Looked at the light. Left. Light unaffected. Keeper unaffected." The ink is smudged around "unaffected."
TIDE: It was not unaffected.
* Close it gently. -> END

== logbook_write
LOGBOOK: The pen is ready. What happened tonight, keeper?
* "Nothing." -> logbook_nothing
* "A letter came." -> logbook_letter
* "Marigold called." -> logbook_marigold

== logbook_nothing
LOGBOOK: Four thousand and eight. Consistent. I admire consistency. Goodnight.
* Goodnight. -> END

== logbook_letter
LOGBOOK: A letter. In eleven years, a letter. I will need a second line for that.
LEDGER: We do not have a procedure for a second line.
TIDE: Make one.
* Leave the pen in the book. -> END

== logbook_marigold
LOGBOOK: There is no column for ships that are not there. I will put it in the margin. The margin is where the true things go.
* Close it. -> END

== logbook_feelings
LOGBOOK: Nobody has asked me that. I am... full. I am nearly full. There are eleven pages left.
TIDE: Eleven pages. Eleven years. It does the arithmetic so you do not have to.
* "We will make them count." -> END

== logbook_feelings_fail
LOGBOOK: I am a logbook. I am logged. Thank you for asking. Please do not ask again.
* Fair enough. -> END

== kettle
KETTLE: ...
LENS: A tin kettle on a paraffin stove. Cold. There is a ring of rust where the water line used to be.
* Light the stove. -> kettle_light
* [LENS 7] Look at the ring of rust. -> kettle_rust | kettle_rust_fail
* Leave it. -> END

== kettle_light
KETTLE: Oh. Oh, that is nice. Nobody has done that since Thursday.
TIDE: It is Thursday.
KETTLE: Then nobody has done that since today. I will boil. It takes a while. Most things worth doing do.
* Wait for it. -> kettle_boil
* Leave it to boil. -> END

== kettle_boil
KETTLE: (a long, rising note, like a ship's horn heard from very far away)
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine... is that you?
LENS: Steam crosses the beam and for a moment the light has a shape: a column, a tower, a person standing very straight.
* Pour the tea. -> END

== kettle_rust
LENS: The rust line is three fingers below the brim, always. You fill it to the same place every night.
LEDGER: Habit.
TIDE: Care. They look the same from the outside.
* Fill it to the line. -> kettle_light

== kettle_rust_fail
LENS: It is rust. On a kettle. It is very much rust on a kettle.
* Leave it. -> END
