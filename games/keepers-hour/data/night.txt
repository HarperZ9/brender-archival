voices: lens 3, ledger 4, tide 2, static 1

== arrive
LETTER: By order of the Coastal Authority, the light at Relay Nine is to be extinguished at dawn. There is no longer a coast to guard.
TIDE: There is a coast. You are standing on it. It just has no sea.
LEDGER: Dawn is at 06:12. That gives us the night. Paperwork first.
LENS: The lamp is turning. It has not stopped in eleven years. Go and look at it.
* Put the letter down. -> END

== lamp
LENS: Four seconds a sweep. The beam crosses the north window, the stairwell, your hands, the north window.
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine, Relay Nine, we see you... kssh...
* Look into the lens. -> lamp_look
* [LEDGER 9] Recite the maintenance schedule from memory. -> lamp_schedule | lamp_schedule_fail
* [STATIC 10] Listen to whoever just said that. -> lamp_voice | lamp_voice_fail
* (?letter) Decide what happens at dawn. -> decide
* Step back. -> END

== lamp_look
LENS: Three hundred and twelve facets of pressed glass. Each one holds a smaller lamp, turning.
LENS: In the middle there is a white point you cannot look at for long. You look at it for long.
TIDE: It is not looking back. Probably.
* Count the facets. -> lamp_count
* Look away. -> lamp

== lamp_count
LENS: You lose count at forty. You start again. You lose count at forty.
LEDGER: The number is three hundred and twelve. It is written on a brass plate. You polished the plate on Tuesday.
* Fine. -> lamp

== lamp_schedule
LEDGER: Wick trimmed daily. Lens wiped at dusk. Clockwork wound every four hours. Log signed at each winding.
LEDGER: You have done all of these, every night, for eleven years, for nobody. That is not nothing. That is a record.
* It is a record. -> lamp

== lamp_schedule_fail
LEDGER: Wick... daily. Lens... at some point. Something about the clockwork.
LEDGER: We had it written down. We always have it written down. The logbook will know.
* Ask the logbook later. -> lamp

== lamp_voice
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine. This is the Marigold, out of nowhere, bound for nowhere. We have been following your light for a long time...
STATIC: ...please do not... kssh... we would not know where we were.
TIDE: There is no Marigold. There has not been a ship here since the water went. And yet.
* "Marigold, this is Relay Nine. I hear you." -> lamp_marigold
* Turn the radio down in your head. -> lamp

== lamp_voice_fail
STATIC: ...kssh... kssh... a shipping forecast for a sea that is not there. Fair, becoming poor. Good, occasionally moderate.
* Let it fade. -> lamp

== lamp_marigold
STATIC: ...kssh...
LENS: Nothing answers. The beam crosses your hands again.
TIDE: You said it out loud. That counts for something. Tell the logbook.
* Step back. -> END

== logbook
LOGBOOK: Ahem. Relay Nine, keeper's log, volume eleven. You are holding me upside down.
LENS: The last entry is in your handwriting. It says "Nothing." in careful capitals.
* Turn it the right way up. -> logbook_open
* [TIDE 8] Ask it how it is. -> logbook_feelings | logbook_feelings_fail
* Close it. -> END

== logbook_open
LOGBOOK: Four thousand and seven entries. Four thousand and six of them say "Nothing." One of them says "Gull."
LEDGER: The gull was in the spring of the third year. We were very excited about the gull.
* Read the gull entry. -> logbook_gull
* Write tonight's entry. -> logbook_write

== logbook_gull
LOGBOOK: "03:40. Gull on the gallery rail. Looked at the light. Left. Light unaffected. Keeper unaffected." The ink is smudged around "unaffected."
TIDE: It was not unaffected.
* Close it gently. -> END

== logbook_write
LOGBOOK: The pen is ready. What happened tonight, keeper?
* "Nothing." -> logbook_nothing
* "A letter came." -> logbook_letter
* "Marigold called." -> logbook_marigold

== logbook_nothing
LOGBOOK: Four thousand and eight. Consistent. I admire consistency. Goodnight.
* Goodnight. -> END

== logbook_letter
LOGBOOK: A letter. In eleven years, a letter. I will need a second line for that.
LEDGER: We do not have a procedure for a second line.
TIDE: Make one.
* Leave the pen in the book. -> END

== logbook_marigold
LOGBOOK: There is no column for ships that are not there. I will put it in the margin. The margin is where the true things go.
* Close it. -> END

== logbook_feelings
LOGBOOK: Nobody has asked me that. I am... full. I am nearly full. There are eleven pages left.
TIDE: Eleven pages. Eleven years. It does the arithmetic so you do not have to.
* "We will make them count." -> END

== logbook_feelings_fail
LOGBOOK: I am a logbook. I am logged. Thank you for asking. Please do not ask again.
* Fair enough. -> END

== kettle
KETTLE: ...
LENS: A tin kettle on a paraffin stove. Cold. There is a ring of rust where the water line used to be.
* Light the stove. -> kettle_light
* [LENS 7] Look at the ring of rust. -> kettle_rust | kettle_rust_fail
* Leave it. -> END

== kettle_light
KETTLE: Oh. Oh, that is nice. Nobody has done that since Thursday.
TIDE: It is Thursday.
KETTLE: Then nobody has done that since today. I will boil. It takes a while. Most things worth doing do.
* Wait for it. -> kettle_boil
* Leave it to boil. -> END

== kettle_boil
KETTLE: (a long, rising note, like a ship's horn heard from very far away)
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine... is that you?
LENS: Steam crosses the beam and for a moment the light has a shape: a column, a tower, a person standing very straight.
* Pour the tea. -> END

== kettle_rust
LENS: The rust line is three fingers below the brim, always. You fill it to the same place every night.
LEDGER: Habit.
TIDE: Care. They look the same from the outside.
* Fill it to the line. -> kettle_light

== kettle_rust_fail
LENS: It is rust. On a kettle. It is very much rust on a kettle.
* Leave it. -> END

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the stairs

== step
STEP: Creak.
LEDGER: Step forty-one. It has creaked since the second winter. There is a requisition form for it, unanswered.
* [LENS 8] Look at the wood. -> step_look | step_look_fail
* [TIDE 7] Step on it again, gently. -> step_again | step_again_fail
* Step over it. -> END

== step_look
LENS: Someone has carved a tally into the riser. Four strokes and a slash, over and over, small, for years.
LENS: It is not your hand. The keeper before you counted something here.
TIDE: Nights, probably. That is what you would count.
* Count them. -> step_count
* Leave them uncounted. -> END

== step_count
LENS: Three hundred and sixty-five, and then one more stroke, much fainter, as if the knife was tired.
LEDGER: A year and a day. The old tenancy term. They served it exactly and then they left.
* Go on up. -> END

== step_look_fail
LENS: Wood. Old wood. Polished by feet into a shallow bowl. It is a step.
* Go on. -> END

== step_again
STEP: Creak. Creak.
TIDE: It is not complaining. It is answering. You knock, it answers. That is most of what a friendship is.
* Knock twice more. -> END

== step_again_fail
STEP: CREAK.
LEDGER: That was louder than necessary. You will wake nobody, because there is nobody, but still.
* Sorry, step. -> END

== painting_one
LENS: An oil painting of this tower, done from the sea, back when there was sea. The lamp is a single dab of white.
STATIC: ...kssh... that is the view from the Marigold... kssh...
* [LENS 9] Look at the dab of white. -> painting_one_light | painting_one_light_fail
* [STATIC 9] Ask the Marigold how it knows. -> painting_one_static | painting_one_static_fail
* Move along. -> END

== painting_one_light
LENS: Up close the white is not white. It is every colour on the palette, laid on in a ring, with a black dot in the middle.
LENS: Whoever painted it knew that a light seen from far off is mostly the dark around it.
* Move along. -> END

== painting_one_light_fail
LENS: It is white. It is a dab. You are not an art critic, you are a keeper.
* Move along. -> END

== painting_one_static
STATIC: ...we were painted in too... look... lower left... kssh...
LENS: Lower left there is a small boat with a yellow sail, and a figure in it, waving at the tower.
TIDE: Nobody has waved at this tower in eleven years. Wave back.
* Wave at the painting. -> END
* Do not wave at the painting. -> END

== painting_one_static_fail
STATIC: ...kssh... kssh...
* Move along. -> END

== painting_two
LENS: A portrait of a woman in a keeper's coat, the same coat as yours, buttons done up wrong.
LEDGER: Agnes Varga, keeper, nineteen-something to nineteen-something. The plaque has been polished blank.
* [TIDE 8] Ask her about the job. -> painting_two_tide | painting_two_tide_fail
* [LEDGER 8] Recall what the register says about her. -> painting_two_ledger | painting_two_ledger_fail
* Leave her be. -> END

== painting_two_tide
TIDE: She would say: you do not keep the light for the ships. You keep it so that you are a person who keeps a light.
TIDE: She would say it kindly, and then she would tell you your buttons are wrong.
LENS: Your buttons are wrong.
* Fix your buttons. -> END

== painting_two_tide_fail
TIDE: She looks past you, at the stairs. She is waiting for someone, and it is not you.
* Leave her be. -> END

== painting_two_ledger
LEDGER: Register, volume three. "Varga, A. Requested the light be kept one night past decommission. Request granted. Light kept. Varga, A. resigned the next morning."
TIDE: One night past. That is all she asked for. That is all we have.
* Remember that. -> END

== painting_two_ledger_fail
LEDGER: There was something about her in volume three. The volume is downstairs. The stairs are long.
* Leave her be. -> END

# ------------------------------------------------------------- the radio room

== radio
RADIO: (a hiss like the sea used to make)
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine... Marigold... still here... kssh...
* [STATIC 8] Tune it carefully. -> radio_tune | radio_tune_fail
* Turn it off. -> radio_off
* Leave it on. -> END

== radio_tune
STATIC: ...this is the Marigold, out of nowhere... we have been at sea since the sea left... we navigate by you...
STATIC: ...if the light goes, we will be the only thing out here that remembers there was water...
LEDGER: There is no frequency in the register for the Marigold. There is no Marigold in the register.
TIDE: The register is not the sea.
* "Marigold, what do you want me to do?" -> radio_ask
* Write the frequency down. -> END

== radio_ask
STATIC: ...kssh... we want what anybody wants... to be told where we are... kssh... and then to be let go...
* Turn the dial slowly back to silence. -> END

== radio_tune_fail
STATIC: ...cricket scores... a recipe for barley soup... an apology, not for you... kssh...
* Leave it on anyway. -> END

== radio_off
RADIO: (click)
TIDE: The silence is very large now. It fills the room the way water fills a glass.
* Turn it back on. -> radio
* Sit in the silence. -> END

== chart
LENS: A sea chart of this coast. Depths in fathoms, rocks marked with tiny crosses. All of it is now dry land, farmed.
LEDGER: Chart 2210, corrected to the edition before last. The corrections stopped when the water did.
* [LENS 8] Find the tower on the chart. -> chart_find | chart_find_fail
* [LEDGER 9] Read the corrections in the margin. -> chart_margin | chart_margin_fail
* Roll it up. -> END

== chart_find
LENS: There: a small star and the words "Fl. 4s", flashing every four seconds. Beside it, in pencil, somebody has added "still".
* Leave it as it is. -> END

== chart_find_fail
LENS: Too many crosses. Too many depths. You find a town that is now a car park.
* Roll it up. -> END

== chart_margin
LEDGER: "Shoal extending, 3 cables." "Shoal extending, 5 cables." "Shoal extending, 2 miles." "Shoal."
LEDGER: And then, in a different hand: "Sea not found. Light maintained."
* Add your initials. -> END

== chart_margin_fail
LEDGER: The handwriting is a keeper's handwriting, which is to say illegible by design.
* Roll it up. -> END

== letters_box
LENS: A biscuit tin full of letters. All addressed to "The Keeper, Relay Nine". All from children, all from years ago.
* [TIDE 7] Read one. -> letters_read | letters_read_fail
* Close the tin. -> END

== letters_read
TIDE: "Dear Keeper, I can see your light from my bedroom. When it comes round I say goodnight to it. Does it say goodnight back. From Ilse, age 7."
TIDE: The town is still there. Ilse is not seven any more. But somebody is.
* Put the letter back with care. -> END

== letters_read_fail
TIDE: The writing is round and enormous. You cannot read it because your eyes have gone strange.
* Close the tin. -> END

# --------------------------------------------------------- the keeper's room

== bed
LENS: A narrow bed, made with hospital corners. You have not slept in it at night for eleven years. Days only.
* [TIDE 8] Lie down for a moment. -> bed_lie | bed_lie_fail
* Not yet. -> END

== bed_lie
TIDE: The ceiling has a crack in it shaped like a coastline. You know every inlet.
LENS: The beam comes in through the slit window above you, every four seconds, and lies across the blanket like a cat.
TIDE: If it goes off, the room will be just a room.
* Get up. -> END

== bed_lie_fail
LEDGER: The clockwork needs winding in fifty minutes. Lying down is how you miss a winding.
* Get up. -> END

== letter
LETTER: By order of the Coastal Authority, the light at Relay Nine is to be extinguished at dawn.
LENS: There is a second page. You have not read the second page.
* [LENS 7] Read the second page. -> letter_two | letter_two_fail
* Fold it away. -> END

== letter_two
LETTER: "The Authority thanks the keeper for their service. The keeper may, at their discretion, retain one item of station equipment as a memento."
LEDGER: One item. Not the lens. The lens is property. Read the schedule.
TIDE: One item. Choose it like you would choose what to save from a fire.
* Think about it. -> END

== letter_two_fail
LENS: The second page is stuck to the first with something that was once marmalade.
* Fold it away. -> END

== photo
LENS: A photograph of you on your first day, holding the keys, squinting. The sea is behind you, just. A thin bright line.
* [TIDE 9] Look at your face. -> photo_face | photo_face_fail
* [STATIC 10] Listen to the photograph. -> photo_static | photo_static_fail
* Turn it to the wall. -> END

== photo_face
TIDE: You look afraid and pleased, the way people look at the start of something long.
TIDE: You do not look like that now. You look like someone who knows how long it was.
* Turn it back to face the room. -> END

== photo_face_fail
LENS: It is a photograph of a person. You recognise the coat.
* Leave it. -> END

== photo_static
STATIC: ...kssh... the sound of a shutter... gulls, hundreds of them... a voice saying "smile, then"... waves...
TIDE: Waves. Listen to that. Waves.
* Listen until it fades. -> END

== photo_static_fail
STATIC: ...kssh...
* Leave it. -> END

# --------------------------------------------------------------- the gallery

== town
LENS: Far off and below, the town: a few dozen lights where the harbour used to be. A car moving slowly. A window going dark.
* [TIDE 7] Think about who is awake down there. -> town_awake | town_awake_fail
* [LENS 10] Look for the bedroom window. -> town_window | town_window_fail
* Look away. -> END

== town_awake
TIDE: A baker. A nurse coming off a shift. Someone who cannot sleep, at a window, watching a light on a tower turn.
TIDE: They do not know it goes off at dawn. Nobody told them. Nobody tells the people who were not asked.
* Look a little longer. -> END

== town_awake_fail
TIDE: Lights. People, presumably. It is hard to feel things about a pixel.
* Look away. -> END

== town_window
LENS: Third street up from the old quay, top floor, a small window. The curtain is open. A shape there, small.
LENS: When the beam crosses the town, the shape lifts a hand.
* Lift a hand back. -> END

== town_window_fail
LENS: Windows. So many windows. Any of them could be the one. None of them has to be.
* Look away. -> END

== railing
LENS: Cold iron, painted white over rust over white. Below it, a long drop to dry grass and a field of sheep.
* [LEDGER 7] Check the railing bolts. -> railing_bolts | railing_bolts_fail
* Lean on it. -> railing_lean

== railing_bolts
LEDGER: Twelve bolts, all tight. You check them every Sunday. It is not Sunday. You checked them anyway.
* Good. -> END

== railing_bolts_fail
LEDGER: One of them turns a quarter. Nothing to be done now. Make a note. You will not make a note.
* Leave it. -> END

== railing_lean
TIDE: The wind comes up off the fields smelling of grass and, faintly, impossibly, of salt.
STATIC: ...kssh... it remembers... the air remembers... kssh...
* Breathe in. -> END

== gull
LENS: The place on the rail where the gull stood, in the spring of the third year. You never repainted that bit.
* [STATIC 11] Call the gull. -> gull_call | gull_call_fail
* Leave the place empty. -> END

== gull_call
STATIC: ...kssh...
LENS: Nothing. Then, out of the dark over the fields, a white shape, too big for the dark, riding the beam.
LENS: It lands on its place. It looks at the light. It does not leave.
TIDE: Log it.
* "Gull." -> END

== gull_call_fail
LENS: You make a noise you believe a gull would respect. The sheep below look up.
* Leave it. -> END

# --------------------------------------------------------- dawn, and the endings

== resume
LEDGER: There is a night already under way. The kettle is where you left it. The voices remember what they said.
* Carry on from where you were. -> @@continue
* Begin the night again. -> @@new

== decide
LENS: 05:58. The sky over the fields is going grey at the edges. The lamp turns. The beam is thinner now against the light.
LEDGER: Dawn at 06:12. Fourteen minutes. The switch is on the wall, where it has always been, under a little brass cover.
TIDE: Whatever you do, do it on purpose.
* Keep it burning. Let them come and turn it off themselves. -> ending_keep
* (?painting_two_ledger) Do what Agnes did: keep it one night past, then let it go properly. -> ending_ceremony
* (?town_window) Turn the lens by hand until the beam rests on the town. -> ending_town
* Not yet. -> END

== ending_keep
LEDGER: You leave the brass cover closed. 06:12 comes and goes. The lamp turns. Nobody comes.
LENS: At 09:40 a van from the Coastal Authority grinds up the track. A young man with a clipboard climbs all 112 steps.
TIDE: He stands in the doorway, out of breath, and watches the lamp for a long time before he says anything.
STATIC: ...kssh... Relay Nine, Relay Nine, we see you... kssh...
LENS: He writes something on the clipboard. Later you will read it: "Light found operating. Keeper found operating. Recommend: review."
* The light is kept. -> @@end

== ending_ceremony
LEDGER: You write the last entry first, so it is done properly: "06:12. Light extinguished by the keeper. Nothing." Then you cross out "Nothing".
TIDE: You wind the clockwork one last time anyway. It deserves to finish its turn.
LENS: At 06:12 you lift the brass cover and turn the switch. The lens keeps turning for eleven seconds on its own weight, dark, and then it stops.
STATIC: ...kssh... thank you, Relay Nine... we know where we are... kssh... and then nothing, the good kind.
TIDE: Agnes asked for one night past. You gave the light one night past. You did not ask for anything. That was allowed too.
* The light is let go. -> @@end

== ending_town
LENS: The lens is heavy and it does not want to stop, but you lean on the brass rail and walk it round, a degree at a time, until the beam lies on the town.
LENS: It stays there. Third street up from the old quay, top floor. A small window, lit up like noon.
TIDE: Down in the town a light comes on, and then another, and then a whole street, people at windows in their coats.
LEDGER: This is not in any schedule. There will be paperwork.
TIDE: At 06:12 you turn it off, and the town, all of it, turns its own lights off a moment later, like an answer.
* The light is given away. -> @@end
