The Keeper's Hour
=================

The last night of a lighthouse on a coast the sea left years ago. About 25
minutes. Walk the tower, talk to what is in it, listen to the four voices in
the keeper's head, and decide what happens to the light at dawn.

Run it
  Double-click keepers-hour.exe. No install is needed.
  The night is saved each time you change room. Next time you start, you
  can carry on or begin again.

Controls
  Walk                    WASD or arrows        left stick
  Turn the view           hold right mouse      right stick
  Talk, continue, choose  E, Space, Enter, click   A
  Pick a reply            1 to 6, or Up/Down then E   D-pad, then A
  Leave a conversation    Esc                   B
  Fullscreen              Alt+Enter
  After dawn              R begins again        Start

A skill check shows its sums: the voice's rating plus two dice against a
target. Failing a check opens a different line, never a dead end.
