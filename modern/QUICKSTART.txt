brview: BRender on a modern PC
==============================

BRender was Argonaut Software's real-time 3D engine of the 1990s. brview opens
the sample models that shipped with BRender 1.3.2 and draws them with BRender
1.4, the modern 64-bit BRender maintained at github.com/BlazingRenderer/BRender.

Run it
  Windows: double-click brview.exe. No install is needed.
  Linux: run ./brview from the unpacked folder. It needs a desktop with
  OpenGL 3.3 (X11 or Wayland) and glibc 2.38 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04,
  Debian 13, Fedora 39 or later). SDL3 is in the folder; nothing to install.
  On Linux, drop the .exe from the commands below: ./brview dat/teapot.dat

  brview.exe                      cycle through the bundled models
  brview.exe dat\teapot.dat       open one model
  brview.exe my.dat my.pix        open your own model with a texture
  brview.exe --force-software     draw with BRender's software rasteriser
  brview.exe --help               every option

Controls
  Mouse drag        orbit          Right or middle drag   pan
  Wheel, Q and E    zoom           WASD or arrow keys     orbit
  Tab or N, P       next, previous model
  T                 texture on or off
  Space             spin on or off
  R                 reset the view
  H or F1           hide the help
  Alt+Enter         fullscreen     Esc   quit

Gamepad
  Left stick orbit, triggers zoom, A next model, X previous, B spin,
  Y texture, Start reset, Back help.
